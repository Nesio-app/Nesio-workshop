The core liquid-glass surface. Wrap any content to float it over the gradient as a frosted panel. Tint with a zone tone to signal which cabin it belongs to.

```jsx
<GlassCard tone="cool" interactive>
  <h3>收纳 · Storage</h3>
  <p>3 件物品即将过期</p>
</GlassCard>
```

`tone`: plain | cool | warm | neutral. `raised` for a resting lift, `interactive` for hover lift + cursor. Works in both day and night themes automatically via the glass tokens.
